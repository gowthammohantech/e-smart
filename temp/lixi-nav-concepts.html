Three ways to fit Home, Sell, Buy, Stock, People, Reports and More around Lixi

Each phone is a working bar at real iPhone size (a 390 pt screen). Tap tabs, tap the orb, and try the dock. The phones use the app's own colour tokens; switch them between dark and light below.

Tip: on concept 1, press and hold an icon to see its name.
