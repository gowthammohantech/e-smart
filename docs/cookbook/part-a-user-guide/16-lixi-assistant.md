# 16 — Lixi assistant

Lixi answers questions from your books in plain language: sales, who owes you, what you owe, stock, spending, GST and e-way bills. It can also prepare a draft or a payment for you, but **it never changes your books without asking**. Every change waits for you to confirm it.

- **Plan:** every plan.
- **Roles:** every role can ask questions. An action Lixi prepares runs with your own permissions; if your role can't do it, Lixi says so.
- **Connection:** Lixi needs the internet. Offline, you see *"I can't reach the server right now, so I can't read your books."* with **Try again**.

### Open Lixi

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| 📱 Any tab | **Hold a tab**: Lixi opens already answering about that tab. On Stock, for example, it asks "What is low on stock?" |
| 📱 Tab bar | **Swipe up on the tab bar** and let go once the orb locks in |
| 📱 Sales plans | Tap the **orb in the middle of the tab bar** |
| 📱 Home | **Pull down on Home**, the way you'd refresh |
| 📱 Anywhere | The **Floating orb** (off by default): drag it anywhere, tap it to open |
| 📱🖥 Menu | **More › Tools › Ask Lixi** |
| 🖥 Top bar | **Ask Lixi** opens a panel on the right, next to the page you're on |

Turn the phone gestures on or off in **Appearance & language › Ways to open Lixi** ([13](13-settings.md#appearance--language)).

### Ask a question

**Steps**
1. Open Lixi. It greets you and flags anything that needs attention, for example *"Heads up: 3 overdue invoices, 2 items low on stock."*
2. Tap a suggestion, or type in **Ask Lixi about sales, dues, GST…** and tap **Send**.
3. Lixi shows **Reading your books…**, then answers with figures and **Open** links to the records it mentions.

**Good questions to try** (the demo is Vertex Traders):

| Ask | You get |
|---|---|
| *How are sales this month?* | Invoiced value, number of invoices, average and amount collected |
| *Who owes me money?* | Total outstanding, how much is overdue, and the biggest late invoice |
| *What do I owe suppliers?* | Payables, what's past due, and what is due in 7 days |
| *Anything wrong with GST?* | IRNs registered, rejections, e-way bills about to expire, documents missing an e-way bill |
| *What is low on stock?* | Items at or below their reorder level |
| *What did I spend last month?* | Expense total and count, plus what you owe suppliers |
| *Top customers* | Your top customers by sales |
| *How much does Kumar Electronics owe?* | That customer's outstanding and overdue invoices |
| *INV/2026-27/0012* | A summary of that document and its status |

Start over with **Start a new chat**. Lixi's sounds can be switched off with **Mute Lixi**.

### Let Lixi prepare something

Lixi can prepare these for you:
- a **draft** invoice, quote, order or bill;
- a payment;
- a new customer or supplier;
- an expense;
- sending a document or a payment reminder.

There are two kinds of preparation:

**It opens a screen for you to fill in.**
- Lixi says, for example, *"I can open a new tax invoice. I won't finalise it for you."* Then it asks **Open "New invoice"?**.
- Tap **Open**. The normal form opens, and you choose what gets saved: *"Lixi only opens the screen — you decide what gets saved."*

**It prepares the change itself.**
- Lixi shows a summary of exactly what it will do: *"Nothing is saved until you confirm."*
- Tap **Confirm** to carry it out, or close the dialog to cancel.

**Rules & gotchas**
- Lixi never **finalises** a document. You always issue invoices yourself.
- A prepared action is valid for about 5 minutes. After that you see *"That expired. Ask me again and I'll prepare it afresh."*
- If the action fails, Lixi says why: *"That didn't go through: …"*. For example, your role doesn't allow it.
- On a sales plan, asking about purchases, stock or expenses gets: *"… isn't part of Smart Basic, so I have nothing to read there."* with **See plans**.
- If Lixi doesn't understand, it lists what it's good at. Try a document number or a contact's name.
