# 03 — Finding your way around

The phone app and the web app have the same screens, laid out differently:
- 📱 **Phone** and narrow browser windows: a tab bar at the bottom.
- 🖥 **Desktop** (browser 768 px or wider): a sidebar on the left and a top bar.

---

### 3.1 The tabs on your phone

The tab bar depends on your plan.

| Plan | Tabs | Where the rest lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Full plans** (Smart Pro, Smart Business) | **Home · Sell · Buy · Stock · More** | People, Reports and GST are in **More** |
| **Sales plans** (Free, Smart Basic) | **Home · Sell · GST · More**, with Lixi's orb in the middle of a floating bar | Customers, Items & services and Reports are in **More › Sales** |

**Tips**
- **Swipe** left or right on a screen to move to the next or previous tab.
- **Hold** a tab to ask Lixi about that screen. You can switch this off (see [16 — Lixi assistant](16-lixi-assistant.md)).
- A red number on **More** counts unread notifications.

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Dashboard: this month's numbers, what needs attention, quick actions ([04](04-home-dashboard.md)) |
| **Sell** | Sales hub: quotations, sales orders, delivery notes, invoices, sales returns, payments in ([05](05-sales.md)) |
| **Buy** | Purchases hub: purchase orders, goods receipts, bills, returns, expenses, payments out ([06](06-purchases.md)) |
| **Stock** | Items, stock levels, movements and stock actions ([09](09-items-and-stock.md)) |
| **GST** | E-invoices, e-way bills, GSTR-1, transporters, GST settings ([11](11-gst-compliance.md)) |
| **People** | Customers and suppliers ([08](08-contacts.md)) |
| **Reports** | The reports hub ([12](12-reports.md)) |
| **More** | Everything else, in groups (below) |

### 3.2 The More menu

| Group | Entries |
|---|---|
| **People & reports** (full plans) or **Sales** (sales plans) | People / Customers, Items & services, Reports |
| **Money** | Receivables, Payables, Payments received, Payments made, Expenses |
| **Tools** | Scan a bill or receipt, Ask Lixi, Global search, Notifications |
| **Business setup** | Business profile, Branches, Users & roles, Taxes, Currencies & rates, Document numbering, Payment accounts, Expense categories |
| **Data & compliance** | GST compliance, GSTR-1, E-invoicing & e-way bill, Transporters, Integrations, Backup & export, Audit trail, Sync status |
| **Account** | Devices & sessions, Plan & billing, Appearance & language, About, **Sign out** |

Entries your plan can't open are hidden. For example, Payables, Payments made and Expenses don't appear on a sales plan. Your name card at the top of **More** opens your profile.

### 3.3 Switch business

**Goal:** Move between businesses on your account, for example from Vertex Traders to Aurora Design Studio.
**Who / plan:** Anyone who belongs to more than one business.

**Steps**
- 📱 Tap the business name in the screen header. The **Switch business** sheet opens. Pick a business.
- 🖥 Use the business switcher at the top of the sidebar.
- On either: **Your profile › Your businesses**.

**What happens:** *Each business keeps its own books, numbering and reports.* Each business's plan decides what you see. That's why Vertex Traders (Pro) shows Buy and Stock, and Aurora (Basic) doesn't.

### 3.4 Switch branch

**Goal:** Work as one branch, or see every branch together.
**Who / plan:** Full plans with more than one branch.

**Steps**
1. 📱 Tap the subtitle under the business name.
2. Pick a branch, for example *Pune — Warehouse*, or **All branches**.

**What happens:** New documents default to the chosen branch, and stock levels show that branch's quantity. **All branches** shows everything combined.

### 3.5 Search everything

**Goal:** Find an invoice number, a customer, an item's SKU, a payment reference or an expense.

**Steps**
- 📱 **More › Global search**. Type into *Invoices, contacts, items, payments…*
- 🖥 Click the search box in the top bar, or press **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** (or **/**). This opens the command palette.
  - Type at least two characters to search pages and your records.
  - **↑↓** to move, **Enter** to open, **Esc** to close.
  - The palette also lists **Quick actions** (New invoice, Receive payment, …) and **Pages**.

**Rules & gotchas:** Searches are not remembered. Search looks only in the business you are in.

### 3.6 Notifications

**Goal:** See what happened: invoices sent, payments received, overdue reminders, stock alerts, e-invoice and e-way bill events.

**Steps**
- 📱 **More › Notifications**.
- 🖥 The bell icon in the top bar. **View all** opens the full list.

Then, as needed:
- **Mark all read**.
- **Clear all**. *The underlying records are untouched.*

**Rules & gotchas:** Notifications appear in the app. Push, email and WhatsApp delivery depend on what is set up under **Integrations** (see [13 — Settings](13-settings.md)).

### 3.7 The web desktop layout

**Sidebar**
- **Home**, then three groups:

  | Group | Sections |
  |---|---|
  | **Transactions** | Sell, Buy, Stock |
  | **Compliance** | GST |
  | **Manage** | People, Reports |

- Each section expands to its pages, for example Sell › Invoices.
- **Collapse sidebar** / **Expand sidebar** switches to icons only; hover over an icon for its pages. The browser remembers your choice. Below 1024 px wide, the sidebar is icons-only.
- **Settings** opens with its own side menu.

**Top bar**
- Search box (⌘K).
- **Offline** and **N failed** badges, when they apply.
- **Ask Lixi**, which opens a panel on the right.
- Notifications.
- Account menu: **Profile**, **Plan**, **Appearance**, **All settings**, **Sign out**.

**What's different on desktop**
- Document lists are sortable tables, with a **Balance due** column.
- The document editor is one page instead of the 4-step phone wizard. **Ctrl/⌘+Enter** finalises.
- **More** becomes a grid of cards.
- **Copy link** copies to your clipboard. **Send by email** and **Send on WhatsApp** open your own mail app or WhatsApp Web.
- Bill scanning (OCR) isn't available in a browser (see [10 — Expenses & bill scanning](10-expenses-and-ocr.md)).

### 3.8 Siri and Shortcuts (iPhone)

On iPhone, Siri and the Shortcuts app can open a prefilled screen, such as a new invoice for a chosen customer. The screen opens ready for you to check and save; nothing is saved without you.
