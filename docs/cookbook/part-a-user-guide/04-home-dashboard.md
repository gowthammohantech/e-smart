# 04 — Home dashboard

**Home** shows how the business is doing this month, and what needs your attention today. It always covers the business and branch you've selected (see [03 — Finding your way around](03-navigation.md)).

---

### 4.1 Read the dashboard

**Goal:** See this month's position at a glance.
**Who / plan:** Every role and plan. Tiles for modules your plan doesn't include are left out.

**Sections, from the top**

1. **Greeting**: *Good morning, Gowtham*, followed by *Here's how the business is doing this month.*

2. **This month** tiles:

   | Tile | What it shows |
   |---|---|
   | **Sales** | Value invoiced this month |
   | **Collected** | Payments received this month |
   | **Expenses** | Expenses recorded this month (full plans) |
   | **Net** | *Sales less expenses* |
   | **E-invoices** | How many were *Reported to IRP*, or *N rejected* in red |
   | **E-way bills** | **Active**, or *N expire in 24 h* |

3. **Sales trend**: *Invoiced value, last 6 months*.

4. **Money owed to you**: **Total receivable**, with *… overdue* and *… due soon*. Tap it to open **Receivables**.

5. **You owe**: **Total payable** and open bills (full plans). Tap it to open **Payables**.

6. **Needs your attention**: *Chase these first.* Possible rows:
   - **Overdue invoices**
   - **Draft invoices**: drafts you never finalised
   - **Quotes awaiting reply**
   - **Items low on stock**
   - **E-invoices rejected**
   - **E-way bills expiring in 24 h**

   Tap a row to go straight to the list.

7. **Recent invoices**: the latest invoices with their status. Tap one to open it.

**Rules & gotchas**
- **Sales** counts finalised invoices only. Drafts and cancelled documents never count toward any total.
- Amounts in other currencies are converted at the rate saved on each document.
- The dashboard is loaded from the server when you open it. Without a connection, what is already on screen stays readable but doesn't refresh.

---

### 4.2 Use quick actions

**Goal:** Start the most common jobs in one tap.

**Steps:** Under **Quick actions**, tap one of these:
- **New invoice**
- **New quote**
- **Receive payment**
- **Add expense** (full plans)
- **Add customer**
- **Add item**

📱 The **Create** button on Home opens the same list.

**Rules & gotchas:** An action your plan can't open is hidden. For example, Aurora Design Studio (Smart Basic) has no **Add expense**.

---

### 4.3 First steps for a new business

**Goal:** Get from an empty book to your first invoice.
**When you see it:** A new business with no invoices shows **Let's get you started** and *Set up in three steps*:

| Step | Do this | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Add a customer**: who you sell to, with their GSTIN and address | [08 — Customers & suppliers](08-contacts.md) |
| 2 | **Add a product or service**: what you sell, with its price and GST rate | [09 — Items & stock](09-items-and-stock.md) |
| 3 | **Create your first invoice**: bill a customer and send it as a PDF, link or WhatsApp | [05 — Sales](05-sales.md) |

The checklist shows *1 of 3 done*, and so on. It goes away once you have invoices, and the dashboard fills in.
